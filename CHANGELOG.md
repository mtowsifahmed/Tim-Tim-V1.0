# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (launch)
- WhatsApp by voice (official links): open WhatsApp or a chat, and send a message to a saved contact after you confirm. Private contact list in Settings with .vcf/.csv import. See WHATSAPP.md.
- New logo and app icons everywhere (website, installable app, Windows installer, Android).
- Installable web app (PWA) with a phone layout; Android app wrapper; Windows installer build.
- Privacy Policy and Terms pages; sign-in screen links to them.
- Settings → Your data: download or copy everything stored about you; delete your account.
- Abuse protection: turns can't be started faster than once a second.
- A phone now registers as a phone (no duplicate messages; reminders, web pages and searches work on the phone).
- Faster replies: fastest free providers first, short caches, quicker first spoken words, brief answers by default.
- Tim-Tim says it was created by Zaftan Studios (change with COMPANY_NAME).
- Tests no longer depend on a private .env; fixed a test that leaked state into other tests.
- Live web search, deep research with sources, 22 computer skills with permissions, reminders, date/time awareness (earlier releases).
