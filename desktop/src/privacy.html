Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This is a plain-language policy for Tim-Tim version 1. It is a template written for this app and is not legal advice. Have a lawyer review it for your country before you rely on it.

Who we are

Tim-Tim is an AI companion made by Zaftan Studios ("we"). Contact: m.towsifoffical2013@gmail.com.

What we store

  • Your email address, used to sign you in with a one-time code (handled by our sign-in provider, Supabase).
  • Your account details: your plan (Free or Pro) and any role we gave you.
  • Your settings: theme, name, personality sliders, language and time zone.
  • Memory notes that you ask Tim-Tim to remember, or add yourself. You can edit or delete them any time.
  • Daily usage counters (how many requests you made today) so we can apply the daily limit on any requests we pay for.
  • The API key you add. It is encrypted on our server, only the last four characters are ever shown, and it is used only to make your own requests to that provider — never for anyone else.
  • A log of computer actions Tim-Tim proposed (for example "open an app" and whether you allowed it). We do not store the contents of files.

What we do not store

  • Voice recordings. Your speech is sent over an encrypted connection to our server and straight on to a speech-recognition provider, then discarded. We do not save audio.
  • Your WhatsApp contact list. Names and numbers you save for the WhatsApp feature are kept only on your own device. Tim-Tim opens WhatsApp chats with WhatsApp's official links; it does not read your WhatsApp messages or status.
  • Your conversations. The last few messages are kept in the server's memory only so the conversation makes sense, and are lost when the server restarts. They are not written to our database.

Who else sees your information

To answer you, Tim-Tim sends the text of your messages (and the text of what you say) to third-party services, which have their own privacy policies:

  • AI language models: for example Groq, Cerebras, Google Gemini, SambaNova, OpenRouter, Mistral and NVIDIA, depending on availability.
  • Speech recognition and voices: for example Groq, Deepgram, Google, Cloudflare and ElevenLabs.
  • Live web search: when a question needs fresh information, your search words go to services such as Tavily, DuckDuckGo, Google News, GDELT and Wikipedia.
  • Our hosting: Render (servers) and Supabase (database and sign-in).

Some free AI services may use inputs to improve their products. Please do not tell Tim-Tim passwords, bank details or other highly sensitive information. We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

Computer actions

Tim-Tim can only do things on your computer (open an app, read or write a file, and so on) in the desktop app, and only after you confirm each action or allow that type of action. These actions run on your device. When you ask Tim-Tim to message someone on WhatsApp, the contact's name and the words you spoke go through our AI services to understand the request; the phone number is looked up on your device and is not sent to us.

Your choices

  • In Settings → Your data you can download or copy everything we store about you, and you can permanently delete your account.
  • You can also email us to ask for access, correction or deletion.

Security

Connections are encrypted in transit and saved API keys are encrypted at rest. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

Children

Tim-Tim is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
